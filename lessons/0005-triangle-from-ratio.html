Lesson 0005 · CLEP Precalculus

The Triangle Inside the Ratio

Given one trig value of an angle, recover all the others — by drawing the triangle the ratio is describing. Two worked examples, then twenty-nine drills.

CLEP hands you a single ratio — sin θ = 3/5, tan θ = 2/3, sin θ = z/10 — and asks for a different function of the same angle. In Section 2 there's no calculator, so the only route is the triangle.

Three moves, every time:

  1. Draw the triangle the ratio describes.
  2. Pythagoras the missing side.
  3. Read off whatever was asked.

sin θ = z/10 says: there is a right triangle whose side opposite θ is z and whose hypotenuse is 10. That is a complete blueprint — the third side follows.
