Lesson 0006 · CLEP Precalculus

Graph ↔ Equation

Lesson 0003 taught the four knobs one at a time. The exam hands you a curve and four equations that each get three of them right. Two worked examples, then thirty-two drills.

Read the features in a fixed order and finish with a check:

  1. Midline: D = (max + min)/2
  2. Amplitude: |A| = (max − min)/2
  3. Period → B: B = 2π/period
  4. Anchor one feature — a peak, or a midline crossing — which fixes the phase and the sign together
  5. Verify: evaluate your equation at one labelled x-value

Step 5 is the one people skip and the one that decides the question. A distractor that agrees with you on midline, amplitude and period will still give the wrong number at x = 0.

Read before drilling: Paul's Online Math Notes — Graphs of Trig Functions. Which feature anchors which function, how the distractors are built, and the equivalent-writings table are all in the sinusoids reference — keep it open beside you.

Before the drills, two rounds on the grapher. It compares curves, not formulas, so any legitimate equivalent counts as a match — which is the point.

Drills

Thirty-two problems in three blocks: read one feature, then anchor phase and reflection, then verify by plugging in. Budget about fifteen minutes and do all three in one sitting — the timer in each block starts on your first answer. At the end of a block, redo only what you missed.
